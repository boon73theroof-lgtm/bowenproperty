About me

Local knowledge. Clear conversations.

I’m Bowen Loo, a residential real estate agent serving Skudai and Johor Bahru. For four years, I’ve helped clients buy, sell, and rent homes, supporting more than 120 property sales and rentals along the way. I work especially well with expat families looking for a comfortable place to settle and investors seeking residential property opportunities. I’ll take time to understand what matters to you, explain each step clearly, and help you make informed decisions with confidence. I speak English, Mandarin, and Bahasa Malaysia, so we can communicate in the language that feels easiest. If you’re planning a move or investment, feel free to get in touch.
